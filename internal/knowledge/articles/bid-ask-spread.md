## Understanding the Bid and Ask

The bid-ask spread is the gap between the highest price a buyer is willing to pay (the bid) and the lowest price a seller is willing to accept (the ask). Whenever you inspect a quote on TradeEdu, you will see both prices alongside the most recent transaction price. If you want to buy immediately with a market order, you pay the ask price. If you want to sell immediately, you receive the bid price.

## Why Spreads Exist

Market makers and liquidity providers require compensation for taking on the inventory risk of holding shares that might decline in value. The spread represents this compensation and acts as a transaction cost borne by participants who demand immediate execution. Liquid large-cap stocks typically feature narrow spreads measured in a few Swedish öre, whereas illiquid small-cap securities can exhibit wide spreads that erode investment returns before a stock even moves.

## Calculating Spread Costs

You can calculate the percentage spread as the ask price minus the bid price divided by the ask price. For instance, if an instrument has a bid of 99.50 SEK and an ask of 100.00 SEK, the 0.50 SEK spread represents a 0.50% immediate hurdle. A position must gain more than the spread just for the trader to break even after purchasing at the ask and selling at the bid.
