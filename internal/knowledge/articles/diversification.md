## What is Portfolio Diversification?

Diversification is the risk management practice of allocating investment capital across different securities, sectors, asset classes, and geographic regions. The central goal is to combine assets that do not move in perfect lockstep with one another. By holding diverse positions, adverse events that severely damage one specific company or industry will have a contained impact on the broader health of your total portfolio.

## Systematic versus Unsystematic Risk

Investors face two primary categories of investment risk: systematic risk and unsystematic risk. Systematic risk represents macroeconomic factors such as inflation, central bank interest rate decisions, and geopolitical crises that affect all market assets simultaneously. Unsystematic risk refers to company-specific dangers, such as executive fraud, product failure, or supply chain disruptions. Diversification effectively eliminates unsystematic risk without sacrificing expected portfolio returns.

## Implementing Practical Diversification

In a practical portfolio, owning twelve to twenty stocks spanning sectors such as industrials, clean energy, technology, and consumer staples captures most statistical benefits of diversification.
