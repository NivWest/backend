## What is Maximum Drawdown?

Maximum drawdown measures the largest percentage decline in an account's total equity value from a historical peak to a subsequent trough before a new peak is reached. It serves as an essential indicator of downside risk and capital vulnerability. While profit charts highlight upside potential, maximum drawdown reveals the maximum pain and paper losses an investor experienced during turbulent market episodes.

## Calculating Drawdown Percentages

Drawdown is calculated as the dollar loss from the highest recorded equity peak divided by that peak equity value, expressed as a negative percentage. If your initial balance grows to a peak of 120,000 SEK, subsequently declines to 90,000 SEK during a market correction, and later recovers to 130,000 SEK, the drawdown experienced was negative 25.00%. The calculation does not conclude until a new all-time high is established.

## Psychological and Recovery Implications

Large drawdowns require disproportionately massive percentage recoveries just to get back to breakeven. A 50% loss requires a 100% gain simply to restore initial capital. Psychologically, deep drawdowns frequently trigger emotional panic selling at the exact bottom of market cycles.
