## Understanding Opportunity Cost in Finance

Opportunity cost represents the potential benefits an investor forfeits when choosing one financial course of action over an alternative. In investment management, capital is finite: allocating 10,000 SEK to a speculative tech stock means those exact funds cannot simultaneously earn risk-free interest or purchase undervalued dividend stalwarts. Every investment decision implicitly involves rejecting competing opportunities for that same capital.

## The Cost of Holding Uninvested Cash

Holding excessive unallocated cash in a trading account carries a subtle but substantial opportunity cost. In modern financial systems with positive benchmark interest rates, idle cash loses real purchasing power to inflation every single month. In TradeEdu, the platform provides "The Vault", an educational high-yield feature that allows traders to deposit idle SEK cash to earn simulated risk-free APY. Leaving capital unallocated outside the Vault incurs an immediate opportunity cost equal to the forgone risk-free yield.

## Balancing Liquidity with Yield

While cash offers ultimate liquidity and flexibility to seize sudden market dips, holding 100% cash indefinitely ensures zero real capital growth. Successful investors strike a deliberate balance: they keep sufficient liquid reserves for tactical entries while sweeping remaining idle balances into safe, yielding instruments to ensure every krona works continuously.
